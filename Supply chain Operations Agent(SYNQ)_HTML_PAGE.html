01 Customer context

When every delay creates a chain reaction.

Consider a fictional regional logistics operator managing a distributed fleet and time-sensitive deliveries. Its operations desk receives signals from vehicles, inventory systems, traffic conditions and shipment updates—but those signals are spread across different views.

FICTIONAL CUSTOMER CONTEXT

“We can see that something is going wrong. The hard part is knowing what matters first, why it is happening, and what action is safe to take.”

Time-sensitive operations

⌁

Fragmented signals

Asset, traffic, inventory and delay indicators need to be considered together.

◎

Accountable decisions

Operators need traceable evidence and a human review step before consequential action.

02 Business challenge

Move from alert overload to operational clarity.

The challenge is not simply detecting a delay. It is turning available operational data into a consistent, explainable response workflow.

TODAY · CHALLENGE

Reactive exception handling

Manual triage across separate reports, inconsistent prioritization, repeated investigation and limited visibility into why an alert was raised.

TARGET · OPPORTUNITY

Evidence-led decision support

One operational view that highlights risk indicators, shows the supporting records, suggests next steps and records human review.

03 Solution

Supply Chain Operations Command Center.

A unified experience for monitoring, investigating and reviewing logistics exceptions. Deterministic business rules calculate risk; conversational AI helps users explore the evidence.

▦

Monitor

Bring key operational indicators and exceptions into a single command view.

⌕

Investigate

Trace risk signals to observed fields, thresholds and source records.

✓

Review

Present recommended checks and capture an operator's decision before action.

06 Implementation journey

Built in practical, testable stages.

A phased delivery approach keeps the operational experience useful even while enterprise integrations are being completed.

Data foundation

Inspect the dataset, validate field definitions and load the approved source into Fabric or the backend data layer.

Decision service

Implement deterministic risk rules, evidence references, API endpoints and transparent limitations.

User experience

Build the command center, investigation journey, what-if controls and human review workflow.

AI & validation

Connect Fabric Data Agent and Copilot Studio, test grounded answers, secure endpoints and rehearse the demo.
